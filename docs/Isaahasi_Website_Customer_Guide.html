1. The big picture in one page

Your website is a normal public website, with a small "back office" behind it that takes donations, saves form submissions and emails receipts. There is no separate admin panel to log in to on the website itself. Instead, you manage things through four outside services, each with its own login:

RazorpayTakes the money (UPI, cards, net banking) and pays it into the NGO's bank account.Razorpay Dashboard
Google SheetsYour "database". Every form submission and payment record appears as a row in a spreadsheet.Google Drive / Sheets

VercelThe "landlord" — hosts the website, runs the back office, stores the secret settings, runs the daily monthly-receipt job.Vercel Dashboard

Visitor's browser→Website (hosted on Vercel)→Back office (Vercel)

Back office→Google Sheet (records)

3. What happens when someone donates

The donation window (opened by any Donate button) has two tabs: DONATE ONCE and DONATE MONTHLY. The donor picks ₹500, ₹1,000, ₹5,000 or types any amount, and fills in: Full Name, Email, Mobile (+91), Address, Nationality (Indian / not Indian) and PAN (checked for correct format).

A. One-time donation

  1. Donor fills the form and presses Donate. The website asks Razorpay to create an order.
  2. The Razorpay payment window opens (UPI, card, net banking, wallets).
  3. Donor pays. Razorpay sends a signed confirmation back.
  4. The back office checks the signature (this prevents fake "paid" claims).
  5. A receipt PDF is made on your receipt template (with the authorised signature) and emailed by Resend to the donor, copied to info.isaahasi@gmail.com. Subject: "Your donation receipt — RCPT-…".
  6. The donor sees: "Your donation was successful. A confirmation has been sent to your email and mobile number."
  7. A record is added to the Payments tab of the Google Sheet.

B. Monthly donation

  1. Donor chooses DONATE MONTHLY, fills the form and approves a recurring payment in Razorpay's window (card or UPI AutoPay / e-mandate).
  2. The first month is charged immediately. The back office adds a row to the Live Subscriptions tab and sends the first receipt straight away.
  3. Razorpay charges the donor automatically every month (set up to run up to 120 months ≈ 10 years, unless cancelled).
  4. Every day at about 9:00 AM IST an automatic job on Vercel (the "cron job") checks every live subscription, finds any month that was paid but not yet receipted, emails the receipt ("Your monthly donation receipt — installment #N") and records it in Payments.
  5. When a subscription is cancelled/completed/expired, its row is automatically removed from Live Subscriptions (the history stays in Payments and in Razorpay).
So: a monthly donor's receipt for months 2, 3, 4… arrives by email within about a day of the charge. It is automatic; nobody has to do anything.

4. The outside services — who does what

4.1 Razorpay — payments

  • Does: shows the payment window, collects the money, settles it to the NGO bank account, runs the monthly auto-debits, issues refunds.
  • You use it to: see every payment, check settlements into the bank, refund a donation, cancel a monthly subscription, download reports for your accountant.
  • Two modes: Test (pretend money, for practice) and Live (real money). A toggle at the top of the dashboard switches between them. The website must use Live keys to take real donations.
  • Needs from the NGO: completed KYC (PAN, bank account, registration documents) — done through Razorpay's own dashboard.

4.2 Google Sheets & Google Cloud — the records

  • Does: stores every volunteer, partner, newsletter and payment record in one Google Spreadsheet.
  • Behind the scenes: a Google Cloud "service account" (a robot Google user) writes to the sheet. The sheet must stay shared with that robot's email as Editor — if you remove it, no forms or payments will be saved.
  • You use it to: read enquiries, export to Excel, follow up with volunteers and partners, reconcile donations.

4.3 Resend — emails

  • Does: delivers the receipt email with the PDF attached, from your own domain.
  • You use it to: check whether a particular receipt was delivered, bounced, or never sent.
  • Needs: your domain verified inside Resend (a one-time DNS setup by whoever manages the domain).

4.4 Vercel — hosting and the "engine room"

  • Does: serves the website to visitors, runs the back-office code, stores the secret settings (keys and passwords) safely, and runs the daily receipt job.
  • You use it to: see if the latest version is live, read error logs, check the daily job ran, update a setting, see usage and billing.
  • The project is called isaahasi-frontend.

4.5 Smaller items

  • Google Fonts — free; the browser loads the typefaces from Google. Nothing to manage.
  • Domain name — your website address is rented yearly from a registrar (GoDaddy, BigRock, Namecheap, etc.). It must be renewed, or the site goes offline. Your registrar's DNS settings also hold the records that let Resend send email from your domain.
  • Source code (GitHub or similar) — where the website's code is stored. Keep ownership with the NGO, not an individual.

8. Everyday tasks

A. Check today's donations (2 minutes)

  1. Razorpay → switch to Live → Transactions → Payments → filter Status = Captured.
  2. Optionally cross-check the Payments tab in the Google Sheet.

B. Cancel a monthly donor's subscription (on their request)

  1. Razorpay → Subscriptions → search the donor/subscription ID.
  2. Open it → Cancel subscription (choose "cancel immediately" or "at end of current cycle").
  3. No further charges happen. The row disappears from Live Subscriptions on the next daily run.

C. Refund a donation

  1. Razorpay → Payments → open the payment → Refund → full or partial. Razorpay does not charge a fee for the refund itself (but the original fee is generally not returned — confirm with Razorpay).
  2. Note: refunds are not emailed by our system; inform the donor yourself.

D. Add a new compliance document to the website

This needs the developer (or someone comfortable with the code), because the list is part of the website:

  1. Put the PDF file in the folder public/compliance/.
  2. Open src/data/complianceDocuments.ts and add one line { name: "…", file: "…pdf" } in the right section, newest first.
  3. Publish (push to the main branch); Vercel automatically rebuilds the site in about a minute.
  4. Check on the live /compliance page that the file opens.

E. Publish a change to the website

Changes to text, images or pages are made in the code by the developer and uploaded to the code repository. Vercel notices and publishes automatically. Watch it under Vercel → Deployments (Building → Ready). If a deployment fails, the previous good version stays live.

F. Practise with test mode

  • Razorpay Test mode uses separate "test" keys and fake money — nothing is charged.
  • Use Razorpay's published test card/UPI details from their documentation.
  • The site shows test or live behaviour depending on which keys are saved in Vercel — do not leave test keys in production.

G. Run the monthly-receipt job right now (if you don't want to wait until morning)

Easiest: Vercel → Project → Settings → Cron Jobs → click Run next to send-subscription-receipts. The developer can alternatively call the endpoint with the CRON_SECRET. The result shows counts such as processed, receiptsSent, failed. Running it twice is safe: a receipt is never emailed twice.

H. Download a report for audit / accountant

  1. Razorpay → Reports → choose Payments / Settlements and a date range → download.
  2. Google Sheet → Payments tab → File → Download → Excel.

11. Security and privacy — what to protect

Rules for everyone

  • Never email or WhatsApp secret keys, passwords or the Razorpay Key Secret. Never post them in screenshots.
  • Turn on two-step verification on Razorpay, Google, Resend, Vercel, your domain registrar and the code repository.
  • Use accounts owned by the NGO (a shared office email like info@…), not a single person's private email, so access survives staff changes.
  • Donor PAN, address and phone are personal data. Limit the Google Sheet's sharing to the few people who need it; remove access when someone leaves.

Points to fix or be aware of (for the developer and the board)

#IssueRecommendation
1A "debug-env" page is publicly reachable at /api/debug-env. It lists the names and lengths of the secret settings (not the values) to anyone who visits.Delete it before launch. It leaks information useful to attackers.
2PAN and address are stored in Razorpay's "notes" and in the Sheet.Keep access restricted; consider whether storing PAN is needed beyond tax-receipt purposes; follow India's DPDP Act obligations (consent, purpose, deletion on request).
3Resend is a US company and keeps email logs for about 30 days on the free plan (receipts, which contain donor names).Mention this in the Privacy Policy; DPDP considerations apply to cross-border processing.
4Receipt PDFs are not password-protected.Acceptable for receipts; just be aware an email forwarded can be read by anyone.
5The Razorpay webhook cannot be reliably edited on the existing dashboard setup, so payment records in the Sheet may miss entries from some events.Use the Razorpay Dashboard as the source of truth for money; the Sheet is a convenience copy. Set up a Live webhook properly at launch.
6One Razorpay "plan" is created for every monthly donation.Harmless but clutters Razorpay → Plans. A future improvement can reuse plans per amount.
7Instagram/LinkedIn icons in the footer do not go anywhere; "Terms of Use" is not clickable.Add real links/page.
8Vercel Hobby plan is non-commercial.Use Pro (Section 5).
If you suspect a secret key was exposed (posted in chat, in a screenshot, in a public place): in Razorpay → API Keys, regenerate the key; update it in Vercel; redeploy. Do the same for Resend and Google keys.
